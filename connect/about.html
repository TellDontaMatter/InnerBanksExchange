About
IBX

Inner Banks Exchange is a small local project for North Carolina's Inner Banks: a community board, a help-wanted and services list, and a shop with flat prices.

What this is

  • IBX Connect is the community board: events, youth programs, local resources and neighbors helping neighbors.
  • IBX Help is the list of help wanted and services offered.
  • IBX Shop sells secondhand and local goods. Each item is one of a kind, graded and priced flat.

Contact

Questions, corrections, or a request to remove a post or item:

opsteadyhome@outlook.com

Please include a link to the post or item you mean. We read every message and reply as soon as we can.

How the board is moderated

  • New posts and comments are reviewed before they appear.
  • Listings are for the Inner Banks. Posts from nearby Suffolk or Hampton Roads appear only when they serve Inner Banks residents.
  • We remove posts that harass or threaten people, are scams or spam, offer anything illegal, or share private details about other people, especially children.
  • Anyone can use Report on a post. We look at every report and remove posts that break these rules.
  • Posts are hidden automatically after 60 days.
  • A listing is not an endorsement. Check details yourself before you rely on a post, and call ahead about hours and dates.

Privacy

There are no accounts, no ads, and no analytics or tracking tools on these sites. Here is what we do keep:

  • Posts. The county, type, title, details and contact information you type. Once a post is approved it is public, so leave out anything private. If we reject a post, we delete it.
  • Comments. An optional name and your comment. Public once approved.
  • Reports. A short note, used only to review the post.
  • Your browser. It remembers the time of your last post, on your device only, to space out posts.
  • Shop payments. Checkout is handled by Stripe. We never see or store your card number. Stripe's own privacy policy covers what you enter there.
  • Where it lives. The sites and the database run on Google Firebase. Fonts load from Google Fonts, and the weather comes from the National Weather Service.

To have a post, comment or item removed, email the address above.
