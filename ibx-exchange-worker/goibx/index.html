        Historic Edenton and the counties around it

        Edenton was one of North Carolina's first colonial towns, and the history is still standing. A few places worth the drive.

        • Edenton

          Cupola House

          Built in 1758 and still standing in Edenton's historic district.

          Photo: Harvey Harrison · CC BY-SA 3.0

        • Edenton

          Chowan County Courthouse

          The 1767 courthouse on the Edenton green, often called one of the finest colonial courthouses in the country.

          Photo: Leah Rucker · CC BY-SA 3.0

        • Edenton

          St. Paul's Episcopal Church

          A colonial-era church in Edenton, begun in 1736. Photographed by Frances Benjamin Johnston.

          Photo: Frances Benjamin Johnston · Public domain

        • Edenton waterfront

          The Barker House

          The home of Penelope Barker, who organized the Edenton Tea Party in 1774. Today it is the town's visitor center.

          Photo: Leah Rucker · CC BY-SA 3.0

        • Edenton

          The Edenton Tea Party

          In 1774, 51 women of Edenton signed a pledge to boycott British tea, one of the earliest organized political actions by women in the colonies. A teapot marks the spot.

          Photo: Frank C Jones · CC BY-SA 4.0

        Local Issues: Data Centers

        Local governments in the Inner Banks are deciding how to handle data center proposals. This section lists the public hearings that have been reported, with the source and the date of each report. IBX Connect takes no position.

        Edenton Town Council public hearing

        Tuesday, Nov 10, 6 p.m.
        Council Chambers, 504 S. Broad St., Edenton

        The hearing is on a proposed 60-day moratorium on data centers. It would pause new zoning approvals, special use permits and site plan approvals for data centers. Ordinary businesses with their own servers are not covered. No complete application is shown as pending.

        Watch by Zoom: meeting ID 252 482 2155, passcode 458434

        Chowan County Commissioners hearing

        Monday, Nov 23, 5:30 p.m.
        Public Safety Center, 305 W. Freemason St., Edenton

        A public hearing on a county pause on data centers. The reporting does not state how long the county pause would last.

        Earlier: Edenton zoning rules

        Monday, Oct 26

        The Edenton Town Council voted unanimously to pull the proposed data center zoning rules.

        Questions neighbors ask

        Who runs IBX Connect?

        Inner Banks Exchange is a small local project for North Carolina's Inner Banks. It is run by locals, for locals. There are no accounts, no ads and no tracking tools. See the About page for contact details.

        How do I post an event or a request?

        Open "Post to the board," pick your county, type and details, and add a phone number or email. Posts that pass the automatic checks go live right away. Web links aren't allowed in posts.

        What if I need help right now?

        Dial 2-1-1 for free, confidential help finding food, housing, health care and other local services, or use the local resources above.
