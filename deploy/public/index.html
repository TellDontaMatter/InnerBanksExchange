The tag standard

Every item has a lot number, a size and a price. When we've graded an item, its grade and a plain note about any flaws are on the tag too.

  1. A

    Clean and ready

    No wear worth mentioning. Cleaned, treated or repaired where needed, and good to use the day it arrives.

  2. B

    Honest wear

    Normal signs of use such as light fading, small scuffs, or patina. Photographed and written down so nothing surprises you.

  3. C

    Priced for the work

    Visible damage or a project piece. The price reflects the flaw and the flaw is the first thing on the tag.
